I'm Andrew Powell — Drew to everyone — a programmer, data analyst and web developer working in Chicago. A Computer Science degree, twenty-five years of shipping systems, and a career spent on both sides of the fence: school districts, a marketing company, a warranty company, and now the Chicago Housing Authority.

              
Most of that time has gone into the unglamorous part that actually decides whether software works: getting data to land in the right place on time, wiring up the integrations between systems that were never designed to talk, and building the page somebody opens on their phone to do their job.

              
This site is hand-written — one HTML file, one stylesheet, one small script. No framework, no build step, nothing between you and the pixels.

Daily

              
A daily check-in app with beacons. One press a day keeps a streak, and every check-in pools a point into a circle you share with everyone on the same name. Boards rank players, circles and countries; the page keeps working offline, and a small ColdFusion API on MariaDB does the shared part.

                04
                ↗
              

              
Candy Tower

              
A building game for my seven-year-old. Tap a shape from the tray, stack it into a pastel candy office tower, and tap any piece to spin or remove it. The shop swaps eight shapes in and out, birds and traffic keep the street busy, and there is no timer, no score and no way to lose. One HTML file, no build step.
